# The paper's sections, in the front page's order.
# One line each: the name, a colon, what belongs. Edit freely; eight at most.

Hungary: the day's biggest stories in Hungarian politics, economy and public life, each as the best free article on it
World: the day's biggest stories in the United States, the European Union, Palestine and China; for China also local reporting and essays, translated whole
Institutions: how rules shape behaviour: incentives, norms, law, bureaucracy and organisations as mechanisms; institutional and behavioural economics; a law or a ruling only when the piece shows what it makes people do
Mind: cognitive science, metacognition, calibration and confidence, reasoning, behavioural science; research and essays near the thesis
Design: product design and UX: interaction, visual design, typography, research, design systems; also systems design; practitioner pieces with a worked case before opinion
Field: the field of the week from fields.md: its primer and its primary text
Football: Brighton and Brentford first, then the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and the Champions League: how a team plays, how a club is run, the numbers behind a match; a weekly dossier on Brighton and on Brentford; analyses and dossiers in English
Film: up to three arrivals a week in Hungarian cinemas ("Ezen a héten a mozikban" on mozipremierek.hu) and on Netflix and HBO Max (mozipremierek.hu/streaming/<year>/?provider=netflix and ?provider=max; series and new seasons from Telex After's column, telex.hu/cimke/streamingajanlo), chosen by taste.md and not in seen.md; RTL+ left out until there is a source for it
